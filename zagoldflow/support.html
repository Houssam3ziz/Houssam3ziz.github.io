FAQ

  • How do I subscribe? Subscriptions are arranged directly with ZA TECH via WhatsApp; we then create the shop and owner accounts. There are no in-app purchases.
  • Can I use the app without an account? Yes — gold prices and the Zakat calculator are available to everyone from the sign-in screen.
  • Forgot your password? Tap “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen, or ask your shop owner to reset it.
  • Why does the app ask for location and camera? Only for employee attendance (to confirm presence at the shop, plus a selfie if the shop enables it); the camera is also used to scan item QR codes.
  • How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.
